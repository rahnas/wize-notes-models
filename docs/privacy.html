Privacy Policy

Wize Notes for iOS and Android · Effective 1 October 2026

Wize Notes records conversations and turns them into transcripts entirely on your device. We do not run servers that receive your audio or transcripts, we have no user accounts, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

What the app stores, and where

  • Audio recordings and transcripts are created and stored only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. They are never uploaded by the app. They leave your phone only if you share them yourself (for example with the Share button) or through your own device backups.
  • Settings (languages, recording preferences, the identifier of your paired pendant) are stored on your phone.

Voice profiles (who said what)

To label transcripts with speakers' names, the app turns short stretches of speech into "voice prints" (numbers describing how a voice sounds) using a speech model that runs on your phone. A voice profile is created only from the voice sample you record during setup (optional) and from speakers you choose to name in your recordings. Voice prints and profiles are stored only on your phone, are never uploaded, and are used only to label your transcripts. You can rename or delete any profile in Settings → Voices; deleting the app deletes them all. If you name other people, please make sure you have their permission where the law requires it.

Microphone and Bluetooth

  • Microphone — used only while you have started listening, to record conversations. On iOS the orange microphone indicator, and on Android a persistent notification, show while it is active.
  • Bluetooth — used only to connect to a compatible Bluetooth recording pendant and receive its audio. On Android, Bluetooth scanning is never used to determine your location.
  • Speech recognition — on iOS, English transcription uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. Other languages use speech models that run on the device.

Network use

The app connects to the internet only to download speech models and the list of available models from github.com/rahnas/wize-notes-models (GitHub) and, for some models, Hugging Face. These are ordinary file downloads: they send no audio, transcripts or personal data. Like any web request, the hosting provider can see your IP address; see GitHub's and Hugging Face's privacy statements.

Data collection and sharing

We collect no personal data and share nothing with third parties. Wize Notes does not track you across apps or websites.

Recording other people

Laws about recording conversations differ by country and state. You are responsible for telling people you are recording and getting consent where required.

Deleting your data

Delete individual recordings in the app (swipe or the ⋯ menu → Delete). Uninstalling the app deletes all recordings, transcripts and downloaded models.

Children

Wize Notes is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: see the support page.
