Wize Notes Support

Private, on-device conversation recorder and transcriber for iOS and Android.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue at github.com/rahnas/wize-notes-models/issues.

Common questions

Does anything get uploaded?

No. Recording and transcription happen on your phone. The app only downloads speech models. See the privacy policy.

Which languages are supported?

English and Malayalam today. Pick them in onboarding or Settings → Languages. More languages can be added without an app update.

Why is the Malayalam model so large?

It is a high-accuracy speech model (874 MB) that runs fully offline. It downloads once over Wi-Fi and stays on your phone.

Do I need the Neo 1 pendant?

No. You can record with your phone's microphone or a connected headset. The NeoSapien Neo 1 pendant is optional for all-day recording. Wize Notes is independent and not affiliated with NeoSapien.

A recording didn't start or stop when I expected

Recording starts when speech is detected (background noise like fans is ignored) and ends after the silence interval set in Settings → Recording (default 2 minutes).

The pendant battery shows "≈"

The Neo 1 doesn't report a battery percentage, so the app estimates it from the pendant's battery voltage.
